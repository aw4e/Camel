# Camel brand

The mark is a small robot whose face is the notch: a dark screen with two pill eyes. The body is a wide capsule, the
shape of the bar itself, 1.62 times as wide as it is tall. It carries one low, round hump (the name), a round ear at each
end and two feet. Ears and feet are part of the mark: every version keeps them, at every size.

| Sky | `#7CC4E8` | 124 196 232 | Eyes |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255 255 255 | Wordmark on dark |

Copper on white is about 3.8:1, enough for a large graphic but not for text. Never set copper as text color; use notch
black or white for words.

## Type

The wordmark is Open Sans SemiBold (SIL Open Font License), converted to outlines, so no font is needed to use it.
Do not retype it in another face.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Keep clear space of at least the width of one eye (about 1/10 of the symbol width) on every side.
- Full symbol: 48 px minimum. Below that, use `logo-small.svg`.
- Small symbol: 16 px minimum.
- Horizontal lockup: 96 px wide minimum.

## Do

- Use the dark-background lockup on the notch black or any dark surface.
- Use the one-color versions for single-ink printing, stamps and embroidery.

## Don't

- Don't recolor the body, change the eye color, or add outlines, shadows or gradients.
- Don't stretch, rotate or crop the symbol, or remove the ears, feet or hump.
- Don't place the full-color symbol on copper, orange or brown backgrounds.
- Don't put copper text on white.

## Notes

- This is a custom drawing, not a font glyph or a traced logo. It has not been through a trademark search; run one
  before registering or using it commercially.
- The SVG sources are generated by hand-written paths: no filters, masks, live text or embedded images.
