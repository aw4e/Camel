## Camel 0.4.0: a channel mixer, lyrics in the closed notch, a calmer layout

### Highlights

**Mixer (new page, under Media).** A channel mixer in the style of Elgato Wave Link, without a driver. Playing apps are grouped
into System, Music, Browser, Voice and Game channels. Each channel has a fader, a level meter and a mute button, and there is a
master strip. Drag an app's name onto another strip to move it; Camel remembers it. Helpers such as Camel itself and `audiodg`
are hidden, and a browser with many audio processes shows once. The list of known apps is plain data in
`assets/mixer-apps.json`, so adding an app is a one-line change. The mixer follows the default output: plug in headphones and
it switches. Type `diagnostics` in Spotlight to copy a report of your outputs and audio sessions for a bug report.

**Mix to (experimental).** Settings, Media and data, *Mix to* plays the sound of every app through an output you choose, with no
virtual device or driver. Camel captures each playing app with the Windows process loopback API, mixes them and plays the mix on
the chosen output. Make an output nobody listens to (an unplugged second jack) the Windows default, pick your headphones in
*Mix to*, and the Mixer faders keep working. It adds about 30 ms of delay. This is the groundwork for per-channel effects; for
now everything is one stream. Needs Windows 10 build 20348 or newer. Please report crackling or dropouts, with the two outputs
you used.

**Lyrics in the closed notch.** While a song with synced lyrics plays, the closed notch shows the current line in the middle,
at the size of the clock, and grows to fit it (up to 380 px; a longer line gets smaller, then is cut). It comes back to the
clock when the song is paused, between lines, or while the focus timer runs. A lyric shift you set with Shift+click on a line
is now remembered per track instead of being lost when the song changes.

**Calmer layout.** The tab bar is grouped (Media, Time, Shelf, System) and the chosen tab shows its name next to its icon.
Weather and the quick toggles (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and the rest) share one roomier Controls panel. Settings are split into
General, Media and data, and Alerts. Page changes no longer flicker.

### Also new
- Spotlight converts money: `100 usd to idr`, `50 eur in jpy`. Rates come from the European Central Bank (via Frankfurter), are
  cached for six hours and fetched in the background.
- The temperature shows in the closed notch, left of the clock.
- Weather is on by default, and a settings file from an older version gets it switched on once.

### Fixed
- Camel's Quit button no longer overlaps the controls button on the Settings page.
- Page changes no longer show one frame at full brightness before the fade starts.

### Internal
- The source is grouped into `app`, `services` and `features`.
- Settings now carry a version number so a changed default can reach old files once.
- Security: system tools (`shutdown`, `schtasks`) run from absolute System32 paths, and the updater re-verifies the new exe
  after the swap.

### Known limits
- The mixer sets the volume of each app, as Windows' own volume mixer does; it cannot create a second stream mix without *Mix to*.
- Mix to mixes all apps into one stream, and its sound quality has only been measured, not listened to on every output type.
- Lyric sync can still be off for some players (issue #2). If it is, Shift+click the line you hear once; Camel remembers it.
