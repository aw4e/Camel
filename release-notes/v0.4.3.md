## Camel 0.4.3: accent colour, Bluetooth battery, smoother lyrics, Japanese and Korean text

### New
- **Accent colour.** Pick one of eight colours under Settings > General. It tints the tab bar, the switches, Spotlight's
  selected row, the Controls tiles and the Stats graph.
- **Bluetooth battery.** The Bluetooth tile shows the connected device and its battery level (red at 20% or less). Windows
  gives one level per device, so there is no left, right or case split.
- **Japanese, Korean and Chinese.** Lyrics, titles and artists in these scripts are drawn with the fonts Windows ships. The
  fonts are loaded only when such text first appears, so Camel stays small otherwise.
- **Camel icon.** `camel.exe` now carries the Camel icon in Explorer, the taskbar and Alt+Tab.

### Lyrics
- A line that is too long wraps onto more rows, centred, instead of shrinking or being cut. The closed notch grows to fit it.
- The notch no longer widens and narrows on every line: it grows at once and shrinks only after a few quiet seconds.
- Lines crossfade, and the player and the lyrics view fade into each other.
- The next line appears on the exact moment it starts, not on the next 33 ms tick.

### Fixes
- **Spotlight** no longer stalls while it looks up icons. They load in the background and appear a moment later.
- Closing the open panel no longer leaves an empty black box for a split second: the content fades out while the shape shrinks.
- The Stats usage graph is filled under the line. Spotlight's selected row shows what Enter does, with a return key.
