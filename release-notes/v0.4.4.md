## Camel 0.4.4: lyric change fix

### Fixes
- The lyric line no longer prints the old and the new line on top of each other when it changes. The old line fades out, then the new one fades in.
- Invisible characters in lyrics (zero-width and direction marks, soft hyphen, BOM, tag characters) are dropped, so they no longer show as a coloured box in the middle of a line.
