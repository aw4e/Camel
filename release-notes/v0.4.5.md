## Camel 0.4.5: smoother lyric changes

### Fixes
- Lyric lines now really fade. Until now the old and the new line were both drawn at full brightness, so they printed over each other when a line changed.
- A lyric change scrolls like a reel: the old line leaves upwards and fades, the new one rises in from below. The two lines never touch, and the text slides away under the clock row.
- A longer line fades in only after the notch has grown wide enough for it, so it is no longer revealed bit by bit.
- The notch shrinks 1.5 s after a long line instead of 5 s, and only when the next line is clearly shorter (24 px or a whole row), so it does not breathe on every line. Thanks to @luciaans for the idea in #4.
